Privacy Policy for Stracko

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Stracko ("the App") is a personal health, fitness, study, and budget tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

No data collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Local storage only

All information you enter into the App — including exercise logs, body measurements, tuition records, food entries, planner items, and expense records — is stored only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

No accounts

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identifying information.

No third-party sharing

Because no data is collected or transmitted, no data is ever shared with third parties.

Data deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App, or by clearing the App's storage/cache from your device's app settings.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
